# Demo Walkthrough Script — Luxury Lead Qualifier (60 seconds)

**Setup:** open the public demo page (`/`), start screen recording. Click
"Fill: 🔥 Hot buyer" so the form is ready. Speak naturally — timings are
approximate.

---

**[0:00–0:08] Hook**

> "In luxury real estate, a $3M buyer inquiring at 11 PM gets the same slow
> response as a casual browser. By morning, they're touring with your
> competitor. This pipeline fixes that — in 60 seconds."

*Action: hero + the two-column demo layout visible.*

**[0:08–0:20] The inquiry**

> "Here's a private-viewing request. Watch what happens the second it's
> submitted — no human needed yet."

*Action: click "Submit test inquiry →". Stage 1 (capture) and Stage 2
(qualification) animate.*

**[0:20–0:35] Transparent scoring**

> "Every buyer is scored 0–100 — budget, timeline, cash versus financing,
> intent — and every reason is shown. No black box. This one's a 100: cash
> buyer, this week, Palm Jumeirah."

*Action: scroll to the score card; point at the "Why this score" reasons.*

**[0:35–0:48] Follow-up + agent alert**

> "Hot buyers get an instant WhatsApp response and a same-day call task — plus
> this: the agent gets pinged on WhatsApp with the full picture, in under a
> minute. Warm and cold leads get their own lighter sequences. Nobody rots in
> an inbox."

*Action: show the follow-up timeline and the mock WhatsApp alert card.*

**[0:48–0:60] The principle + honesty**

> "The rule is simple: automation qualifies, humans close. And everything you
> just saw is simulated in the browser — a concept demo, not a client result."

*Action: scroll to the "Automation qualifies. Humans close." section, then the
honest simulated-vs-real breakdown. End.*
